The chips currently known to be supported by this cdef
(the one for LITTLE ENDIAN platforms)
and the required environment definitions:

- generally:
  CDEF_FLOAT_SUPPORT should be defined if:
    - the chip contains an FPU
    and
    - the system/project is supposed to use floating point math
  or
    - a special exception exists from the architecture rule
	  that only with an available FPU floating point should be used

- VMCARM (Micronas CDC320x)
  _CPU_32BIT_

- FJ16_ST3 (Fujitsu F2MCLLX)
  _CPU_16BIT_

- V850 (NEC JCP 2005, NEC JCP 2011, NEC Fx3)
  _CPU_32BIT_

- MIPS (Toshiba CapM, CapL, CapA)
  _CPU_64BIT_
  
- Jade (Jade1, JadeD)
  _CPU_32BIT_

- Freescale (iMX6)  
  _CPU_32BIT_
